Steps

timers run on this computer too
1

Salt the water like the sea

5 min

Bring a large pot of water to a rolling boil, then salt it well — this is the only moment the pasta itself gets seasoned.

2

Render the guanciale slowly

8–10 min · low heat

Cut into 1 cm strips. In a cold pan, over medium-low heat, let the fat render until the pieces are golden and crisp at the edges. No oil needed.

3

Beat yolks with Pecorino off the heat

3 min

In a bowl, whisk the yolks with the grated cheese and a generous amount of pepper until thick and creamy. scaled: 6 yolks, 120 g cheese

4

Pull the pan off the heat, then combine

2 min

Off the heat. Add the pasta, a ladle of starchy water, then the egg mixture. Toss fast — you want silk, not scrambled eggs.
