    Your recipes, everywhere

    Phone and computer. Even offline.

    Recipes sync automatically — including from offline. Each platform uses the ways you already know: hotkeys on the computer, standard gestures on the phone. On the phone it runs as a PWA: install it on your home screen and you won't tell it apart from other apps.

    Careful handling of your data

    We're paranoid about your privacy.

    So paranoid that the app works without sign-up. We don't know your email or your Google login. Even Telegram is only for saving recipes — don't want it? Don't use it.

  • ·No sign-up required — we don't know your email or Google login
  • ·No trackers, no product analytics
  • ·We never let AI models train on your recipes
  • ·Data export and an API — we don't hold your data hostage
  • ·More details: recipe-scaler.ru/#/privacy
